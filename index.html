    1. 01Book an estimate

      Pick a time online, or call (631) 476-4665. Tell us what happened and who your insurer is.

    Shop policy

    Read before you book.

    • Free estimates. An estimate costs nothing, and you don't have to do the repair here.
    • Book ahead. Someone will be free to look at your car when you pull in.
    • Insurance. Bring your insurance card and claim number if you've opened a claim.
    • Pay at pickup. Your deductible or any balance not covered by insurance is due before the car is released.
    • Can't make it? Call (631) 476-4665 so we can give the time to someone else.

    Frequently asked
    questions

    Do I need an appointment for an estimate?

    Booking online or by phone is the best way to make sure someone is free to go over your car with you. If you're nearby and in a pinch, call (631) 476-4665 first.

    Do you work with my insurance company?

    Yes. Bring your claim number and your adjuster's contact and we handle it from there. New York law lets you choose the shop that repairs your car.

    How long will the repair take?

    It depends on the damage, insurance approval and parts. You'll get a time frame with the estimate, and the tracker page shows each stage as we update it.

    Where are you?

    15 Old Middle Country Rd, Coram, NY 11727, a few minutes from Medford. Get directions.

    What are your hours?

    Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:45 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

    How do I follow my repair?

    Every booking comes with a 6-character repair code. Enter it on the Track your repair page to see the current stage, notes from the shop, and any receipts or photos we've sent.
